# Security

## Scope

BidRoom is a decision-record validator and workflow scaffold. It does not submit bids, contact buyers, price work, or approve spend.

## Reporting

Use the repository's private security advisory feature for vulnerabilities. Do not open a public issue with credentials, personal data, or live procurement material.

## Data handling

- Keep real RFP, bidder, pricing, and employee data out of the public repository.
- Use fictional or synthetic data in examples and tests.
- Redact local usernames, hostnames, tokens, and message links from public evidence.
- Do not put credentials in room documents or policy files.

## Validator limits

The validator checks structure, arithmetic, policy thresholds, and human-ownership fields. It does not prove that evidence is true, complete, or current. Human review remains required.
