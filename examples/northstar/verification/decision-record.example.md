## Rationale

The base bid is not viable: ISO 27001 is expired, only four of five references are available, the lead architect is unavailable for months 1-3, and the base score is 50/100. Helix Systems can cover the ISO, reference, and delivery-lead gaps, raising the score to 68/100, but the partner margin is 11% against a 15% policy minimum and the deadline conflict remains unresolved. The human pursuit lead therefore chose a conditional bid with partner, subject to written evidence, a VP Finance margin exception, and buyer deadline clarification.
