## 1. Background and objectives

The Department of Citizen Services supports more than 40 state programs that residents access through websites, forms, phone lines, and in-person service centers. Residents currently navigate multiple portals, inconsistent eligibility language, and separate appointment systems.

The Department is seeking a partner to design, build, and operate the **Northstar Citizen Services Portal**, a unified digital front door for the Department's highest-volume services. The portal should help residents:

- Find the right program and service without knowing the Department's internal structure.
- Complete common applications and renewals online.
- Understand eligibility, required documents, and next steps.
- Track the status of applications and appointments.
- Request accessibility accommodations and language assistance.

The Department's objectives are to:

1. Reduce avoidable application errors and incomplete submissions.
2. Improve completion rates for high-volume services.
3. Meet or exceed WCAG 2.2 AA accessibility requirements.
4. Provide a maintainable platform that Department staff can operate after launch.
5. Establish a reusable service pattern for future programs.

The Department expects the selected partner to work collaboratively with program staff, residents, accessibility advocates, and the Department's internal technology team.

## 2. Scope of work and deliverables

The selected partner will provide the following scope. The Department may refine the scope during discovery, but the mandatory requirements in §3 may not be waived.

### 2.1 Discovery and service design

- Inventory at least six priority services and document current-state journeys.
- Conduct at least 24 resident interviews, including residents with disabilities and residents with limited English proficiency.
- Facilitate at least four co-design sessions with Department staff.
- Produce a service blueprint, information architecture, and content model.
- Identify policy or process changes needed to support online completion.

### 2.2 Experience design and content

- Design responsive web experiences for desktop and mobile.
- Produce plain-language content for at least six priority services.
- Provide a reusable design system aligned with the Department's brand.
- Provide annotated wireframes and prototypes for usability testing.
- Conduct at least three rounds of usability testing, including assistive-technology users.

### 2.3 Platform build and integration

- Build the portal on a modern, maintainable web platform.
- Integrate with the Department's identity provider for resident authentication.
- Integrate with at least three back-office systems through documented APIs.
- Provide an administrative console for program staff to update content and service rules.
- Provide audit logging for administrative actions.
- Provide a documented API layer for future services.
- Support English and Spanish at launch, with a content architecture that can add additional languages.

### 2.4 Accessibility and inclusion

- Meet WCAG 2.2 AA for all resident-facing pages and flows.
- Provide an accessibility conformance report before launch.
- Test with at least two screen-reader and keyboard-only configurations.
- Provide an accessibility statement and a feedback channel.
- Provide accessibility training for Department content editors.

### 2.5 Launch and transition

- Provide a phased launch plan for at least six services.
- Provide operational runbooks, monitoring, and incident procedures.
- Train Department staff on content, administration, and support workflows.
- Provide a 90-day hypercare period after launch.
- Transition ongoing operations to the Department or a jointly agreed support model.

### 2.6 Ongoing support

- Provide 24/7 support for priority-one production incidents.
- Meet a one-hour response target for priority-one incidents.
- Provide monthly service reporting, including uptime, incident trends, and accessibility defects.
- Maintain a product roadmap and release cadence.
